# Calgym marketing kit

Everything to present Calgym: logos, screenshots, store graphics, social posts, copy and the brand guide. English and Arabic throughout. Made 30 September 2026; screenshots, store graphics, social images and the brand guide's screens updated 8 October 2026 with the redesign (workout card, dish photos, new workout screen).

Start with **calgym-brand-guide.pdf**: it shows the whole brand on 11 pages.

## What's inside

| Folder | What | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| `calgym-brand-guide.pdf` | Logo, colours, fonts, tone of voice, features, plans, screens, taglines | Sharing with designers, partners, press |
| `copy/calgym-marketing-copy.md` | All text, English and Arabic: taglines, pitch, features by module, plans and prices, store listings, screenshot captions, headlines, social posts, partner messages, press text, FAQ, and what we can and can't claim | Copy and paste |
| `logo/svg/` | Vector logos: scale to any size, for print and web | Printers, designers, websites |
| `logo/png/` | The same as PNG, transparent background | Documents, slides, social |
| `logo/app-icon-original/` | The real app icon (gradient with the 3D mark) at 1024, 512, 256, 180 and 120 px, plus the Android adaptive layers | Anywhere the app icon appears |
| `screenshots/plain/` | Clean app screens, 10 per language | App Store / Play upload, website |
| `screenshots/captioned/` | The same screens with a headline on the brand gradient | App Store / Play listing, ads, posts |
| `google-play/` | 512 px app icon and the 1024 × 500 feature graphic (English, Arabic) | Play Console store listing |
| `social/` | Instagram posts (1080 × 1350) and stories (1080 × 1920), English and Arabic | Social media |

## Logo files

Four colours for each kind of logo:
- **original:** gradient mark with dark text. For light backgrounds.
- **white:** for the gradient, purple, black backgrounds and photos.
- **black:** one-colour print.
- **purple:** one-colour brand use.

Kinds:
- **calgym-mark-*:** the symbol alone.
- **calgym-icon-*:** the symbol on a rounded app tile. `original-square` has no rounding: Google Play and Android round it themselves.
- **calgym-logo-horizontal-*:** symbol plus "Calgym".
- **calgym-logo-stacked-*:** symbol above "Calgym", for square spaces.
- **calgym-logo-arabic-*:** symbol plus "كالجيم" (PNG).
